# Seed 0.2.6 physical-device feedback — iteration 1

Implemented 18 November 2026 from `seed-0.2.6-device-feedback-1.md` after Dima's explicit “implement”.

## Changes

- Free relocation of existing non-campfire buildings. Long-press begins a snapped preview; a valid drop commits atomically, and an invalid drop/cancellation restores the original. Visible silhouettes and ground footprints are hittable. The moving building is faded at its original site and previewed over green/red footprint cells. Pan waits for hold failure; pinch cancels a live relocation preview. Inspection also offers **Move building**, with map tap and Confirm/Cancel as an accessible alternative. Campfire remains the immutable geography/exploration anchor.
- Shared placement-site validation enforces bounds, discovered footprint, open terrain, occupancy excluding the moving building, and fishing's revealed-water requirement. No construction cost or technology charge is used for moving. IDs, level, recipe, worker priority, timers, rewards, stocks and save v5 remain intact. Geographic output bonuses derive from the new location immediately.
- Compact resource icons and quantities, with no default resource names/rates. Popups show names, actual gross/use/net rates, capacity, currently producing building count, recipe/building sources, inputs, research gates and known deposits. Current-source counts exclude construction and zero-output buildings. All twelve resources fit on tablet; phone retains horizontal scrolling and accessibility labels.
- Native horizontal building carousel and category scrolling, search and lock information. Removed placement arrows. Goals start collapsed, toggle by tap, and use a tested era-identity observation hook for future expansion; no new era was added and readiness is not treated as an era change.
- Native storyboard launch screen and matching in-app loading views. Save restore and capped offline simulation run in a detached Swift task; loading continues until the renderer finishes its first prepared frame. No artificial delay. Async sessions wait for the presenting scene's initial activation, so a startup suspension uses the offline cap exactly once. Synchronous initialization remains available for existing tests/callers.
