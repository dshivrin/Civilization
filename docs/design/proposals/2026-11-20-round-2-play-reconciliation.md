## Authority and intended result

The owner approved strict operational path connectivity and exempted the civic center's founding food (+2/min), water (+3/min) and housing (+5). Buildings can be constructed before connection. Completed buildings must explain inactivity and how to connect. This supersedes RD-002's purely decorative behavior only for the approved connectivity scope; road styles remain, and no transport bonus, new charge or reward is implied.

This document proposes the exact boundary and behavior. It does not approve bridges, new balance, perimeter/outpost predicates or every system in the source brief. Earlier completed chapters, objectives, rewards, pillars and readiness remain earned. The present assignment authorizes preparation, commit/push and review of this document, not gameplay implementation.

## Current implementation and conflicts

- `Types.swift`: `PlacedBuilding.isOperational` currently means construction finished. Keep that structural meaning for historical witnesses and serialization; add a separate engine-owned operating assessment rather than changing this property globally.
- `Roads.swift`: cardinal neighbor geometry and three free road styles exist. Roads reject water, hills, forest, hidden tiles and building overlap. They are not an access graph; there is no production bridge or gate implementation.
- `Economy.swift`, `Workforce.swift`, `GameEngine.swift`: production, worker allocation, housing and storage currently depend on construction completion, not roads. Storage is clamped during simulation: lowering effective capacity without a retention rule can delete stock. Connectivity must also affect reserve/guidance checks, not just displayed rates.
- `SaveStore.swift` supports envelopes v1–v6; `GameSession.restore` applies normal offline catch-up. The new rule cannot silently reinterpret the pre-activation interval. Review mode already excludes offline catch-up.
- `GameSession.finishDragging/finishRelocation` commits on release. `WorldView.pinch` cancels a placement. Both conflict with the requested explicit candidate confirmation and usable camera gestures.
- `ResearchView` already selects/expands a focused civic prerequisite, but does not set its scroll target. General navigation must carry the exact target and originating context rather than labeling every return as civic-center navigation.
- `SettlementScene` adds a mint production dot at y = −8 for active non-civic buildings; this is the leading cyan-dot hypothesis, not a visually confirmed diagnosis. Clearing terrain uses brown/ochre ground and soil detail; inspect the actual reported area before removing terrain or changing geography.
- `BuildingGeometry.swift` supports captured base dimensions and orientation. Existing production sizes stay unchanged; adding rotation is distinct from approving resized catalogs or new directional artwork.

## Approaches considered

1. **Recommended: separate UX slice, then review-copy connectivity pilot, then explicit normal-save activation.** Produces independently reviewable improvements, exercises safe operating rules without touching normal progress, and exposes transition effects before activation.
2. Enable strict connectivity for every existing save at first launch. Simpler UI, but surprises returning players, changes offline interpretation and risks stock loss unless the entire transition contract ships together. Not recommended.
3. Deliver bridges, local extraction, defenses and revised chapters together. Addresses more feedback at once but requires several unapproved product/economy decisions. Defer.

The recommendation is Codex's proposal; the owner has not selected these detailed policies yet.

## Proposed connectivity contract

- One undirected four-neighbor network with a virtual civic root joining all road tiles sharing a full logical edge with the civic footprint, including branches touching different sides. No diagonal links and no building-to-building or under-building routing. All three road styles conduct identically.
- A completed ordinary building has access when a road in that rooted component shares an edge with any tile along its occupied footprint boundary. Touching a corner is insufficient. Discovery, terrain and occupancy remain authoritative.
- The civic center is the root and needs no road to provide its own cultural output; its existing staffing/research/level rules still apply. Founding provisions remain independent of connectivity and staffing as approved. This whole-civic root treatment is a proposed exception beyond the explicit founding exemption.
- Unfinished buildings retain construction timers and can complete disconnected; they provide no ordinary service until completed and connected. No new construction penalty or fee.
- Completed disconnected producers use no workers, recipe inputs, maintenance or deposit reserves and generate no ordinary outputs. Population food/water consumption continues unchanged. Reconnection restores eligibility; existing labor/input/deposit limits still apply.
- Ordinary housing and storage service require connection. Disconnection never removes population or previously stored stock. Over-cap stock remains spendable; positive production cannot increase it until below capacity. No clamping on tick, reload, switching, construction rewards or review resource grants. Validate retained stock against a separate structural ceiling including all completed storage and earned technology capacity, connected or otherwise; do not accept arbitrary unlimited balances. Lower housing stops further growth; it does not evict inhabitants.
- A scout-required expedition needs a completed connected scout camp at dispatch. Removing its path after dispatch does not cancel, recharge or invalidate an already paid expedition; it completes normally. This proposed ordinary-service rule leaves existing costs, durations and discovery geometry unchanged.
- Path tiles provide connectivity without needing their own connection status or staff. Decorations have no operating service and do not gate routes. Palisades currently have no new enclosure/defense rule. Bridges/gates are future graph connectors only after their geometry, availability and costs are approved; no water routing is faked by road tiles.
- Civic relocation and road edit commits first settle elapsed time under the old topology, then atomically recompute access. Candidate previews and unconfirmed roads never alter live production. Cancel and failed commands preserve topology and resources.
- Derive access from authoritative geometry; do not save a potentially stale per-building connected flag. Recompute on committed topology/discovery/construction changes, not each rendering frame; benchmark the bounded 3,072-tile map and dense layout.
- UI reason precedence: construction, disconnected, then existing labor/input/deposit shortage. Show “Not connected · connect a path to the civic center” with actions to focus the building/open existing path tools; no hidden-terrain leak.

## Proposed transition and preservation contract

1. Implement/test the policy in isolated fixtures and explicit gameplay-review copies first. The shared persistent rules marker, custom envelopes and inspector support must ship in R2-06 before the pilot can be accepted across reloads. Normal saves retain current semantics until the transition is approved and implemented. A Debug pilot is not completion of normal-game connectivity.
2. Propose a versioned rules marker in a v7 envelope, separate from map identity and review metadata. Legacy v1–v6 load with legacy operating semantics; current valid history migrates without replaying rewards or treating disconnected historical buildings as corrupt. Pilot activation is an explicit action on an existing/new review copy, never inferred from its filename or review status.
3. New games use strict rules once the normal activation slice is approved. Existing games show an explicit one-time activation explanation with affected-building counts; declining leaves that game on legacy rules. No automatic conversion on opening a game or making a review copy.
4. At activation, settle elapsed time once using the source rules and existing offline cap, preserve a validated byte-identical source backup, prepare and validate the strict state, then atomically publish it. The marker prevents catch-up/activation/reward duplication. Backup/write/validation failure keeps original selection, bytes and rules. Failed preparation cannot discard progress accrued in the current session.
5. Retain buildings/IDs/footprints/orientations/levels, roads/styles, stocks, population, deposit reserves, discovery, pending construction/expedition, timestamps after the single settlement, and every earned history. No injected roads, grants, deletion, relocation or reset.
6. Each normal/developer/review file owns its marker and backup; activating a review must not activate either companion. Extend the shared inspector and custom developer/review envelopes, not just `SaveStore.save`; reject missing/invalid v7 markers and unsupported future versions. Existing backups remain inspectable without writes or catch-up.
7. Extend immutable history validation before using access in fresh progression checks. Do not replace construction witnesses with current service eligibility. Revised progression predicates are deferred.
