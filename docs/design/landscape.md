# Persistent valley and exploration

Seed 0.2.4 status: APPROVED persistent geography, gradual discovery and technology-dependent identification. Numerical distances, costs and timers are EXPERIMENTAL. The handcrafted 32×24 map, river, hills, forest, clearing, map ID and every deposit origin remain unchanged. No procedural maps or additional landscape regions are introduced.

New settlements initially reveal a Chebyshev radius of 7 logical tiles around the campfire. Narrow known routes lead first horizontally and then vertically to the six known raw deposit origins, allowing the settlement to identify the valley's basic resources without revealing the entire map. Fog hides unrevealed terrain and scenery; discovery persists in saves. An expedition costs 10 food, lasts 45 seconds and expands the revealed radius by 2. Only one expedition may run at once. Expansion beyond radius 11 requires an operational scout camp. The maximum radius is 32, sufficient to reveal this entire map.

The first household expeditions do not consume workforce or require a manufactured resource. Building a scout camp later becomes a concrete exploration choice. Household construction and expedition timers continue offline under the existing eight-hour cap; worker priority changes do not pause them.

Terrain, deposits, discovery and identification are separate data. Extraction requires a revealed, identified deposit origin, while finite reserves retain the economy milestone's persistent quantities. The six initial raw sources are known. Copper remains a future deposit with no extractable Stone Age resource or recipe: its required identification technology is the future identifier `futureMetalworking`. Discovery alone therefore displays an unidentified deposit instead of revealing copper's name or enabling production.

Every tile of a new building footprint must be discovered. Fishing shelters additionally require revealed water within 3 logical tiles of the origin. Existing nearby-terrain output bonuses count only revealed terrain. Other provisional terrain bonuses and extraction radii remain unchanged pending playtesting. A visible buildable bank and a nearby revealed river together make fishing placement understandable; no geography change is needed.

Version-4 saves preserve discovered tiles, current radius and any pending expedition. Versions 1–3 displayed the whole valley, so migration preserves that earlier knowledge by revealing all 768 tiles, setting radius 32 and leaving no pending expedition. Existing resources, buildings, stable IDs, construction, priorities, research, goals and deposit depletion remain intact. Before first replacement, version 3 receives a byte-for-byte `.seed-0.2.3.bak` backup; earlier backup conventions remain unchanged. Invalid version-4 exploration records are rejected rather than silently repaired.

This geography supports continued Stone Age play and future development on the same land. A second age, new landscapes, trade routes and further map generation remain deferred. Reveal pacing, scout threshold, fishing radius and known-route presentation need playtesting; placeholder deposit markers and fog are not final artwork.
