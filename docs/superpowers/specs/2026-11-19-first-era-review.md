This supersedes the prior 0.2.7/proposal-only stop for a review candidate. Permanent artistic acceptance remains Dima's at final review; no intermediate approval pauses are needed for reversible candidate work.

Keep SpriteKit, SwiftUI, native APIs, iOS18 universal landscape, existing signing, fixed geography/footprints/IDs and save v5. No second age, cities, warfare, monetization, weather or speculative systems. Source media examples from City Design Coordination are inspiration only; do not copy unlicensed external reference assets into the app.

Approach: retain the current renderer and make art data-driven/reversible. Generate three original settlement concept alternatives, choose a warm miniature / illustrated naturalistic candidate consistent with the original brief, document the choice as provisional. Generate original transparent building sprites separately, plus original terrain/loading art as useful. Supply native vector iconography where it creates a coherent scalable set; thumbnails derive from the actual building sprites. Preserve an asset manifest, prompts, provenance and world anchors. A real-time 3D rewrite is unnecessary for this candidate; editable code-native geometry/metadata and stable asset names preserve future replaceability. AI raster art is not claimed to be a reusable 3D model.

Candidate deliverables: complete twenty-building visual set and thumbnails; terrain/riverbanks/water/hills/forest/deposits/fog identity; twenty research, twelve resource and five pillar icons; cohesive menus/loading; bounded fire/smoke/water/construction/placement/production effects with reduce-motion support; full normal-cost era and chapter checks; evidence-driven pacing recommendations/tuning only if justified; phone/tablet visual/interaction checks and available physical-device measurements. Existing regressions must remain green, and user saves must stay isolated from test scenarios.

Final review package separates implemented/tested evidence, proposed visual choices and limits requiring Dima's hardware/human review. No claim of subjective fun, physical A15/M1 results without devices, editable 3D models from bitmap generation, or permanent approval. Use local commits for baseline, pipeline/pilot, catalog/world art, polish and verification milestones.
