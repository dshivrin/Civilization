# Seed 0.1 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans. The user explicitly requests implementation and AGENTS.md says to proceed after the short plan.

**Goal:** A playable native landscape Stone Age settlement on iPhone and iPad.
**Architecture:** A local Swift package owns pure Swift domain state, configuration, deterministic elapsed-time simulation, and a separate persistence target. SwiftUI owns HUD and presentation state; SpriteKit renders snapshots and emits tile selections. No economy logic lives in rendering.
**Tech Stack:** Swift 6, Swift Testing, SwiftUI, SpriteKit, Foundation Codable; iOS 18.0 minimum.
**Spec:** ../../..//AGENTS.md

## Global Constraints
- Universal iPhone/iPad, landscape left/right; iOS/iPadOS 18.0.
- Native APIs only; no backend, purchases, or final art.
- Fixed 32×24 map and all tuning in bundled JSON.
- Preserve fractional production; resource cap 500; offline cap 28,800 seconds.
- Atomic build/research commands; stable IDs; one-time goal rewards.

## Review Focus
- Returning from background or moving the clock backwards cannot duplicate production.
- Corrupt/unsupported saves must be reported and preserved rather than silently overwritten.
- Large footprints near edges, forest, water, hills or buildings reject atomically.
- Phone landscape leaves essential controls visible and map gestures isolated from HUD.
- Construction out of goal order still grants each reward only once.
