## UI2-1 · Founder's Lodge and functional opening roads
**Branch:** `riverbend-ui2-founding-review`

Build an isolated fresh-start review: upgrade the starting Campfire **in place** into the Founder’s Lodge, retaining civic identity and logical footprint. Keep the first upgrade affordable/available without any road, remote supply, or research dependency. Use a readable stone-and-timber shelter with a visible doorway and keep clear space around the civic center. After the upgrade, show a free starter footpath and teach the first hut’s route to that entrance. Roads are mandatory for ordinary buildings to operate in this review after founding, not a substitute for valid terrain; the founding civic source itself is exempt. Reuse existing cardinal road/entrance and connectivity semantics rather than introducing a second graph. This does not modify the delivered economy lab rules.

**Done:** A fresh player can upgrade, see both the Lodge and the recommended hut site, build and draw the first connection. With no path, the building explains why it is inactive; connecting it activates service; if disconnected, existing reserves, already paid work and completed history are not erased. At the default camera the Lodge and hut are visible and tappable, with at least one legal visible fallback plot. Verify the disconnected/connected/reloaded path and no ordinary-save or economy regression.

## UI2-2 · One-tap Goals and a usable build catalog

Show purpose, price/shortfall and relevant output once. Provide one primary action for buildable, resource-short or research-locked states: **Build**, **Find materials** or **Open required research**. Route to the exact requirement. Use [Northgard](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/northgard/id1533979882) and [Pocket City 2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pocket-city-2/id1533709428) as mobile references, not assets to copy.

**Done:** Record the actual in-game Goals and catalog flows on smallest supported landscape phone and iPad, including the three action states and return behavior. Verify accessibility labels, target sizes, higher text sizing, safe areas and map gestures against the review build. A detached theme mockup is not completion.

**Branch:** `riverbend-ui2-foothill-review`

Use the existing [roads/palisade/towers/bridges plan](2026-11-20-stone-age-roads-palisade-towers-bridges.md) as reference. Keep out combat, tower batch production, and a general terrain generator.

**Done:** Deliver one integrated review build showing the redesigned founding/menus and the terrain, with a route and enclosure comparison, phone and iPad evidence, passing dependency/no-save-regression checks, and a concise owner playtest guide.
