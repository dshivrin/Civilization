# Stone Age Menus and Navigation Implementation Plan

**Goal:** A new player on an iPhone can tell what to do next, understand what is blocking them, and reach the exact action without scrolling to find it. Build this into normal New Game and Continue, not Developer Tools.

**Baseline:** riverbend-stone-age-hud-review at d3c15ed6fba97080adcebfcb8ad5f5103dacb84a, including completion cues. Source: Dima’s ten iPhone screenshots, IMG_1132–1141. The two “Review Only” walkthrough screenshots are not gameplay rules. On the ordinary screenshots, Build showed Security/Scout Camp with a Stone Gatherer footer and clipping; Research clipped its card and showed a generic deficit; Explore clipped the survey copy; Goals 3/7 sent the player to Explore to find a hidden production recipe. Woodcutter placement feedback used “work faces” without showing which ground was valid. The game had already reached 3/7; the next Woodcutter improvement was the stall.

## 2. Build these four ordinary menus

- [ ] **Goals:** Small map card with current objective, one useful sentence and one labeled action (e.g. “Build Stone Gatherer,” “Open Stone Tools,” “Open Roads,” or “Make Tools”). One tap opens the exact target; never purchases or starts anything. Chapter history is secondary. For multi-step projects show progress and only the current useful step as the main action; expose optional remaining steps separately. Use live state, not a second tutorial or a fixed checklist that can drift from the game.
- [ ] **Build:** Clear, labeled categories and Roads; one scrollable catalog when needed, no nested scrolling. Phone uses a full-height landscape catalog, and selecting an item opens a focused detail view. iPad can retain list and side detail in view. Item title, cost/stock or blocker, and primary action are visible together; extra text is secondary. The selected category/list/detail must always agree, including deep links and filter/search changes. A locked item names and links to its exact technology. Back restores the previous valid context; Done returns to map. Selecting or deep-linking never commits construction.
- [ ] **Research:** Same list/detail and back behavior as Build. Hide empty Stone Age categories rather than showing 0/0 bars. Keep status, cost, and a plain-language deficit visible, e.g. “Need 9 more Knowledge · 6/15.” If missing a prerequisite, name it and link directly. Do not change research duration or costs. Preserve the unread marker and exact-view acknowledgement from the completion-cues goal.
- [ ] **Explore:** First survey opens the exact survey detail. Cost and current state are visible: ready with Send, underway with a countdown, or complete with the result. Do not make the player scroll past secondary entries or disabled buttons. While the old crafting rules still exist, Goals links directly to the relevant Flint/crafting/upgrade control; retain all existing actions and label “one batch at a time,” not “single batch.” Keep secondary findings and actions grouped, not dumped into the required path. For a disconnected building, show the reason and link to Roads from Goals or inspection; retain connectivity rules. Use the current authoritative placement error and readable feedback; changes to legal forest sites and actual art rotation are out of scope.

## 3. Verify and deliver

No nested scroll, clipped text, duplicate dismissals, stale detail, or action overlapping content. Keep tappable areas at least 44 points; support VoiceOver labels and non-color-only states.
- [ ] Use native tests for ordinary New Game, Continue, exact Goals→Build and Research links, changing filters/search, return context, road recovery, survey before/during/after and craft accessibility. Test current crafting through three earned batches and the preserved upgrade in a normal flow wherever feasible; capture data if any native coverage remains incomplete rather than claiming a full run. Assert required actions are hittable *without* scrolling. Coordinate by reading and reusing the separate test-only Goal 3 baseline evidence if it is ready; do not touch its worktree or simulators.
- [ ] Use the established `python3 scripts/dev.py` QA path. Preserve saves, costs, placement rules, completion cues, and gameplay. Commit the plan and deliver a pinned handoff with before/after captures, exact tests, and remaining limits. Work in isolation then only fast-forward the same review branch when clean, tested and safe; no force push, device install, main merge, deployment, or Bronze.

## Approved direction for later goals, not implementation here

Stone Age should have one building per output, with no production switching. Direction approved: Stone Gatherer stays Stone-only; new ordinary Stone Age no longer introduces Flint; a dedicated Toolmaker makes Tools from Stone + Wood without three identical manual starts. Preserve legacy saves, stocks and earned upgrades; balancing and migration are a separate goal. After production, add shared first-time guidance in ordinary play. Actual sprite rotation, legal forest site expansion and research timers are separate gameplay tasks; graphics/NPC appearance comes last. Bronze stays planning-only until Dima’s personal Stone Age approval and explicit go-ahead.
